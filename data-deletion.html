BearClaw Inbox data deletion instructions

Updated October 7, 2026.

  1. Send a private message to @bearclaw.ai saying: "Operator request: delete my BearClaw Inbox data and stop automatic replies." Send the request from the account whose data you want removed.
  2. If you cannot use that Instagram account, privately message the BearClaw Facebook Page, identify your Instagram username, and ask for the operator. The operator may need to verify ownership without requesting passwords or authentication codes.
  3. The operator will handle the request manually, stop admitting that account's new messages to automatic chat, and remove its local delivery records, conversation history, retained conversation-derived memory, and copies under the operator's control. The operator will confirm completion and describe any records that must be retained.

Deleting an Instagram message does not automatically erase every local record. This request does not delete your Instagram account, records maintained independently by Meta, or provider-controlled copies. Use those services' own privacy controls for their records.
