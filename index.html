BearClaw Inbox privacy policy

Updated October 7, 2026. BearClaw Inbox connects the Instagram account @bearclaw.ai to BearClaw, an AI assistant operated for personal conversations with approved friends.

Information processed

Meta delivers message events for this account, including Instagram-scoped sender and recipient identifiers, timestamps, message identifiers, message text, and attachment metadata. During initial sender verification, the setup receiver records only sender identifiers and timestamps. Once automatic replies are enabled, approved friends' messages and conversation history are processed to generate replies. Public Instagram links may be included; receiving an attachment does not mean its contents have been downloaded or watched.

Use and access

Information is used to identify approved friends, maintain conversation context, reply, prevent duplicate sends, and diagnose delivery problems. Local message records are accessible to BearClaw's operator. Private DM text is not intended for public posting or forwarding to group chats. Public content can be shared when requested.

Services involved

Meta handles Instagram messages under its own policies. BearClaw's configured AI services receive conversation content when generating replies. The current configuration uses xAI, with Google Gemini as a fallback. These services process requests under their own policies. Tailscale Funnel relays encrypted connections; webhook TLS terminates on BearClaw's Mac. This service is not entirely local processing.

Storage and deletion

Local delivery records and conversation history can remain until the operator removes them. Access is restricted through local file permissions; this policy does not promise that every store has automatic expiry. Deletion requests cover local delivery records, conversation history, any retained conversation-derived memory, and copies under the operator's control. Copies maintained independently by Meta or AI service providers are governed by those services' policies.

To request access, correction, deletion, or an end to automatic replies, privately message @bearclaw.ai and ask for the operator. See data deletion instructions. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment information, or other sensitive information to this assistant.

Changes

The operator will update this page when the integration's data practices change.
